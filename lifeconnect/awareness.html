Myth vs Fact

❌ Myth: Blood donation causes permanent weakness.

✔️ Fact: The human body replaces donated fluid within 24–48 hours and red blood cells within 4–6 weeks.

❌ Myth: You can contract infections during donation.

✔️ Fact: All collection equipment is sterile, disposable, and used only once per donor.
