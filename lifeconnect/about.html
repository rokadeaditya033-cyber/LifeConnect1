About LifeConnect

Community Engagement Project (CEP) dedicated to saving lives across Maharashtra.

Our Mission & Vision

LifeConnect was engineered as a community-driven web platform to eliminate critical delays in blood transfusion procurement. By connecting voluntary donors directly with patients and hospitals in Pune, Nashik, Ahilyanagar, Shrirampur, Sangamner, and Mumbai, we strive to ensure no life is lost due to unavailability of blood.
